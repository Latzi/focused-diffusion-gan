\noindent\emph{Percent improvements.} For lower-is-better metrics we report relative reductions as $100\times(\text{baseline}-\text{FDGAN})/\text{baseline}$ (DINOv2 FD unless stated). On Cityscapes--Pedestrian, FDGAN reaches a DINOv2 FD of 491.0, 36.6\,\% below PSGAN (774.1) and 40.7\,\% below Projected GAN (828.3); against the plain ADM trained with the same code and budget (754.0) the reduction is 34.9\,\%, of which the box-mask conditioning with guidance accounts for most: FDGAN$^{\ast}$ (505.1), the same model without the adversarial stage, is 33.0\,\% below ADM, and the adversarial stage lowers FD by a further 14.1, i.e.\ 2.8\,\% of the FDGAN$^{\ast}$ value (paired bootstrap difference $-14.1$, 95\,\% interval [-28, -1]; at guidance 1.5: $-32.7$ [-46, -17]; without mask guidance: $-50.1$ [-71, -26]); with a second training seed of both models the same comparison gives $+1.8$ [-14, +17] at 2.0, $+1.5$ [-12, +14] at 1.5 and $-28.9$ [-51, -6] without guidance, so under guidance the adversarial gain in FD is within training-seed variation, while without guidance it is resolved for both seeds (Section~\ref{sec:ablation}). On Traffic Signs, FDGAN (321.2) and FDGAN$^{\ast}$ (315.3) are statistically indistinguishable ($+5.9$ [-4, +14]); both are 45 to 46\,\% below Projected GAN (586.9) and below ADM (587.2), which differ by 0.3 FD. On the COCO potted-plant subset, with the three rows trained with our implementation (ADM, FDGAN$^{\ast}$, FDGAN) initialised from the LSUN-bedroom checkpoint, FDGAN reaches 871.8, 5.9\,\% below DiT-XL/2-G (926.5) and 20.4\,\% below Projected GAN; the difference to FDGAN$^{\ast}$ (883.3) is $-11.5$ [-26, +6], within sampling uncertainty, while KD and the object-level FD favour FDGAN by 6.4\,\% and 1.0\,\%. We therefore attribute most of the gain over the original ADM baseline to the box-mask conditioning with guidance; adversarial training with localized perturbation adds a smaller gain that is resolved against sampling uncertainty for one training seed on Cityscapes--Pedestrian (and for both seeds when no mask guidance is applied), within training-seed variation under guidance when a second seed is trained, and within sampling uncertainty on the other two sets (Table~\ref{tab:decomposition}).
